# Acies rights, release history, and shutdown

Public records checked November 19, 2026.

**Saint Joy Korea Co., Ltd. (㈜세인트조이코리아) is the first contact for the mobile game.** It is the last documented operator of Acies: Battle Runes. Its company profile names representative Choi Seong-ho (최성호) and lists Acies as its flagship title. The public record establishes the operating handoff in 2021. The current legal ownership of the client, server, assets, and trademarks remains unverified. I found no public assignment, acquisition agreement, or later license that settles those rights. [Company profile](https://www.gamejob.co.kr/Company/Detail?M=35298423) · [Archived iOS listing](https://appshunter.io/ios/app/acies-battle-runes/id1515733807/reviews)

## Who to contact

1. **Saint Joy Korea, attention Choi Seong-ho or business affairs.** The GM published **saintjoyhelp@gmail.com** as the primary email in an October 2021 account notice. The company's GameJob profile lists **+82 2-6287-2165**. The email is historical and the GM ended player support on September 12, 2024. Mailbox monitoring and the telephone line remain unverified. The company website did not respond during this check. [GM contact notice](https://cafe.naver.com/acies/111) · [Full notice via Naver API](https://apis.naver.com/cafe-web/cafe-articleapi/v2.1/cafes/30555050/articles/111) · [Company contact listing](https://www.gamejob.co.kr/Company/Detail?M=35298423)
2. **CreaGames Ltd.** Ask its business team who handled the 2020 mobile publishing agreement and whether it can identify the company named in the 2024 shutdown notice. The corporate LinkedIn is a public route. The historical employer profile lists **hr@creagames.ru** as a hiring address, useful only for requesting a referral. [Company LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/creagames-ltd-/) · [Employer contacts](https://career.habr.com/companies/creagames)
3. **BR Games Co., Ltd. (㈜비알게임즈).** The original studio's historical switchboard is **+82 70-7011-4170**. Its 2017 co-representatives were Jeon Yu (전유) and Song Seok-yong (송석용). Ask who took over the code and underlying rights after Tales Arena. [Developer-supplied launch announcement](https://www.inven.co.kr/webzine/news/?news=182950) · [BR Games profile](https://www.gamejob.co.kr/Company/Detail?M=12930410)

For a local-play preservation inquiry, ask which entity can grant written permission for private self-hosting with the existing client, whether it can provide an offline build or a server package, and whether it can separately authorize distribution of a modified client. Ask Saint Joy to forward the request or identify the relevant entity if the rights are split.

## End of service

- **October–December 2023** — The GM posted an October balance patch and iOS 17 fixes. The last archived mobile version is **2.5.1**, dated November 2023 on Android and December 28, 2023 on iOS. The Android mirrors differ by a few days. [Balance patch](https://cafe.naver.com/acies/4954) · [iOS 17 fix](https://cafe.naver.com/acies/4957) · [Android archive](https://www.appbrain.com/app/acies-battle-runes/com.creagames.clashoflegions) · [iOS archive](https://appshunter.io/ios/app/acies-battle-runes/id1515733807/reviews)
- **July 22–August 9, 2024** — The game entered temporary server maintenance on July 22. The August 9 notice still reported connection problems. Contemporary player reviews mention HTTP 503. [July 22](https://cafe.naver.com/acies/5161) · [July 23](https://cafe.naver.com/acies/5174) · [August 9](https://cafe.naver.com/acies/5277) · [Archived player reports](https://chrome-stats.com/d/com.creagames.clashoflegions/reviews)
- **August 12, 2024** — The GM ended service and announced the end of downloads. The overseas publisher's team had encountered a serious operational problem. It worked with the Korean team for about three weeks, then proposed withdrawing its team and ending service. The notice does not identify the underlying problem. Customer support ended on **September 12, 2024**. The company promised full refunds for purchases from June 22 through July 22, with Google Play refunds handled by the company and Apple refunds requested by players. [Official notice](https://cafe.naver.com/acies/5293) · [Full text via Naver](https://apis.naver.com/cafe-web/cafe-articleapi/v2.1/cafes/30555050/articles/5293)
- Both original store listings are unavailable. Trackers disagree on the actual Google Play delisting day in May 2025. The GM's August 2024 date establishes the service end independently. [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.creagames.clashoflegions) · [Apple US listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/acies-battle-runes/id1515733807) · [AppBrain](https://www.appbrain.com/app/acies-battle-runes/com.creagames.clashoflegions) · [Chrome-Stats](https://chrome-stats.com/d/com.creagames.clashoflegions)
